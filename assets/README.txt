CUSTOM AVATAR GUIDE
===================

You can replace the built-in pixel companion with any custom animated GIF or sprite!

Simply place any of the following GIF files in this "assets" folder:
- walk.gif       -> Played while walking onto or off the screen
- idle.gif       -> Played while asking the water reminder question
- celebrate.gif  -> Played when you click "YES" (happy celebration)
- sad.gif        -> Played when you click "Remind me later" (sad / head down)

If any of these GIF files are present, Desktop Companion will automatically 
load and animate them instead of the procedural pixel drawing.

Great sources for free desktop pet / companion sprites & GIFs:
1. itch.io (Search: "desktop pet sprites", "pixel character walk cycle", "tamagotchi sprite")
2. shimejis.xyz or deviantart.com/shimeji (Thousands of Shimeji anime/game characters)
3. opengameart.org (Free pixel art walk cycles)
4. Create your own in Piskel (free in browser: piskelapp.com) or Aseprite!
