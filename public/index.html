🚀 Quick Start & Customization Guide

Tips to get the most out of your companion after installing

🎨 Custom Avatars

Want your own pet? Right-click the system tray icon > Settings... > Avatars > tap "Import Custom Avatar" or drop any animated .gif or .png into the custom_avatars folder. Your pet will automatically load and walk with it!

👑 Accessory Evolution

Stay hydrated to unlock secret items! Reaching 4 cups unlocks pixel sunglasses 🕶️ on your character. Hitting your daily goal unlocks a golden celebration crown 👑 with confetti!

⏸️ Meeting Mode / Pause

In a Zoom call or presentation? Right-click 💧 in your tray (or Mac menu bar) > Meeting Mode / Pause to silence all popups for 30 minutes, 1 hour, or 2 hours. Reminders resume automatically.

🌙 Working Hours & Quiet Hours

By default, companion runs during office hours (Mon–Fri, 9:00 AM – 6:30 PM) and activates Quiet Hours at night (10:00 PM – 8:00 AM) to ensure you are never disturbed when resting.

❤️ Interactive Petting

Click or tap on the walking character anytime to pat it! Floating hearts will burst upward and your companion's mood status will light up with appreciation.

🗑️ Clean Uninstallation

Desktop Companion is fully registered in Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps. You can uninstall it cleanly anytime directly from Windows Settings or your Start Menu with one click.
